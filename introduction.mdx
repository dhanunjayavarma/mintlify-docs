# Welcome to Paperguide

Paperguide is an AI-powered research platform that helps you find papers, conduct literature reviews, manage references, and write academic documents - all in one place.

## What is Paperguide?

Paperguide transforms the research process by combining advanced AI with a comprehensive suite of research tools. Whether you're a student, researcher, or academic professional, Paperguide streamlines every stage of your research workflow from discovery to publication.

## Key features

### AI search
Find research papers and get comprehensive answers backed by citations. Our AI-powered search understands your queries, analyzes millions of papers, and delivers structured answers with proper citations from the most relevant sources.

### Literature review
Conduct systematic literature reviews with automated data extraction. Compare multiple papers side-by-side, extract key information including graphical and tabular data, and verify findings with supporting quotes from original sources.

### Deep research
Generate comprehensive research reports automatically. Paperguide discovers relevant papers, screens them, extracts data, and produces in-depth analysis of research trends, contradictions, and consensus in your field.

### AI writer
Write academic content faster with AI assistance. Generate complete drafts built on your references, polish your writing with AI commands, and maintain academic integrity with automatic citations for all AI-generated content.

### Reference manager
Organize and manage your research references effortlessly. Import references from multiple sources, get AI-generated summaries, annotate PDFs, and collaborate with your team in real-time.

## Access to 200+ million papers

Paperguide provides access to over 200 million open-access research papers, ensuring you have the resources you need for comprehensive research across all academic disciplines.
